# ObsiTube store listing

## Name

ObsiTube

## Short description (under 80 characters)

Stop forgetting YouTube. Highlight the words and keep them in Obsidian.

## Full description

You spend hours on YouTube. A week later, you can't remember a thing.

ObsiTube fixes that. It turns any video into words you can read, keep and see again.

READ EVERY WORD YOU HEAR
• The transcript follows the video, word by word
• Listen in the background, or watch full screen
• Chapters, double tap to skip, and speed control
• Read along from the lock screen

KEEP WHAT MATTERS
• Hold a word to highlight it
• Take exact words, a sentence or a paragraph
• Six colours, plus your own note and tags
• Every highlight links back to its moment in the video

REMEMBER IT
• Old highlights come back in short Review rounds
• Get a daily reminder if you want one
• Find any highlight or note again in seconds

MADE FOR OBSIDIAN
• Use the templates already in your vault
• Each property fills itself in from the video
• See all the video data and choose where it goes
• Works with every property type: Checkbox, Date, Date & time, List, Number and Text
• Mark any property "Ask me" to fill it in every time
• Each box offers the values you already use in your vault
• Change a template in the app or in Obsidian, and the other side follows
• Search your folders, tags and notes as you clip
• Already saved a video? Updating adds to the note and keeps your writing
• Import and export Obsidian Web Clipper settings

KEEP YOUR LIBRARY IN ORDER
• Pick up where you left off, newest first
• Mark videos as done with one tap or a swipe
• A queue, playlists, channels and a feed
• Search titles, channels, tags, highlights and notes

MAKE IT YOURS
• Six app icons, with Amethyst by default
• Ready-made palettes and a palette maker
• Light, Dusk, Dark and true-black OLED
• Your own font, text size and spacing

No account. No ads. Your library stays on your phone, and your notes stay in your vault.

ObsiTube is free software under the GPL-3.0 licence. It is an independent app, not affiliated with Obsidian or YouTube.
