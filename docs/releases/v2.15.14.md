# ObsiTube v2.15.14

## Lock screen

- The lock screen now always starts locked. A phone in your pocket cannot skip, pause or highlight.
- Hold the "Hold to unlock" pill for a moment to use the controls.
- It locks again by itself 10 seconds after your last touch, and when the screen goes off.
- While locked, it shows only the clock, the title and the pill. It no longer draws the whole page behind a cover, so it uses much less battery.
- Fixed: the page no longer draws over the clock and buttons.
