Small changes that do what you expect when you tap.

- Highlights: tap Newest to get Oldest. Tap again to go back.
- Highlights: tap By video to flip that order too.
- Library: pick the sort that is on, and it reverses.
- Home: tap the tab you are on to go to the top, or out of a playlist.
- Reader: tap the time to see how much is left.
- Reader: tap the title to see the video details.
- The lock button shows only while the phone is locked.
