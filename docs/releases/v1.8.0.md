# 1.8.0

- Settings is now a menu. Tap Look, Reading, Highlights, Obsidian, Playback, Lock screen, Updates or Advanced to open that screen. Back goes to the menu. The search box still finds any setting.
- New: Pause when muted. Turn the volume to zero and the audio pauses. Turn it up and it plays again. Settings > Playback and captions. It is on by default.
- Category and tag suggestions now follow what you type. "productit" finds "Productivity". Letters may be skipped.
