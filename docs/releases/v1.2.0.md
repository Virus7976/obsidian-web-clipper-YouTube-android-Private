Big update. Fixes from the phone test, plus offline audio, a queue, playlists and a better library.

New
- Save the audio of a video on the phone. It plays with no network. Per video: ⋮ menu or the reader menu.
- Play queue. "Play next" and "Add to queue". When a video ends, the next one starts. Queue tab on Home.
- Playlist import. Paste a playlist link. It imports into a playlist, not your library. Play all, add all to the queue, save all audio.
- Library tags, categories and sorting. Filter by category or tag. Sort by recent, date added, title, channel, highlights. Long-press a video in grid or compact view for the same menu.
- Always-add tags and properties (Settings > Obsidian > Added to every clip).

Fixes
- The reader no longer overlaps. The player is hidden unless you ask to watch.
- Audio first: play, pause, back 10 and forward 10. "Show the video" is in the menu.
- One "Highlight" button on the text menu.
- Media notification: back 10, Highlight, forward 10.
- Find a word inside one video. Settings has a search box.
- The bottom bar hides while you read. Follow along is in the ⋮ menu.
- Reindex shows what happened. A vault name that differs from the folder name now links.
- Removed whole-vault search and the Vault tab.

Not tested on a phone by the author. Riskiest: wireless ADB pairing, Shizuku, notification buttons, folder reading, the audio download on a slow network.
