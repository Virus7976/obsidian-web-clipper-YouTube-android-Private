# 2.4.0

**Songs, and anything with no captions**
- A video with no captions used to show an error. It now opens a notes page, and the audio plays as usual.
- Start typing a note and it keeps the time you started, not the time you press Add. Mark moment saves just the time.
- Tap a note's time to jump there. Tap the cross to delete it.
- Your notes go into your Obsidian note as timed bullets, such as `- [1:23](link) the bass drops here`.
- The Highlight button on the notification and the lock screen marks the moment when there are no captions.
