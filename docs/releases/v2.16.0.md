# ObsiTube 2.16.0

ObsiTube, ObsiRead and Obsi Bible now work as one set.

- Share any link to any of the three apps. It goes to the right one: YouTube links to ObsiTube, Bible links to Obsi Bible, web pages to ObsiRead.
- Highlights shows your ObsiRead and Obsi Bible highlights too, under "All apps". Tap one to open it in its own app, right at that spot.
- Pick transcript words that name a verse, like "John 3:16", and tap "Open John 3:16" to read it in Obsi Bible. A highlight that names a verse has the same button.
- Settings has "Your Obsi apps": one tap opens the other two, or their download page.
- Only the Obsi apps can read your highlights. Sharing them needs Android 12 or later.
