First release of Marginalia.

**What it does**
- Paste a YouTube link, or share one from the YouTube app.
- Preview the video first, with switches for "Pin player" and "Highlighting".
- Read the transcript in a reader, split by chapters. A red bar marks the line being spoken.
- Hold a line to highlight it. Keep your finger down and drag to take more lines. Hold a highlighted line to erase it.
- Clip to Obsidian with your Web Clipper templates ("Big Idea" and "Default"). Finish with Open Obsidian, Save file, Copy, or Share.
- Audio keeps playing in the background, with a media notification.
- Five looks, plus accent, motion, roundness, reading font and size, templates and vaults in Settings.

**Known limits**
- Not yet tested on a physical phone. Test hold-and-drag highlighting, background play, and the notification first.
- Some videos block playback inside other apps. The reader then shows an "Open in YouTube" button.
- The APK is signed with a debug key. Install it directly. Later releases from this PC use the same key, so they update in place.
