## Marginalia 1.1.0

A big rebuild of playback, highlighting and Obsidian saving.

### Playback
- The video now plays inside the app. No more embed error.
- If one stream fails, the app tries the next one on its own. There are four sources: YouTube's own app clients, NewPipe Extractor, Piped, and Invidious.
- Background playback and the lock-screen controls work through the same player.

### Reading and highlighting
- The transcript is real selectable text. Drag the two handles to take exactly the words you want.
- The toolbar has five colours, plus Sentence and Paragraph buttons.
- The word being spoken is marked as it plays. Tap any word to jump there.
- Settings: what a long-press selects (word, sentence or paragraph).

### Obsidian
- Highlights use Obsidian syntax. Yellow is `==text==`. Other colours use `<mark style="background: ...">`. The colour codes are from memory of the Highlightr plugin, so check them in your vault.
- Add a vault folder in Settings. The app reads your notes, folders and tags.
- Search your vault from the Home screen. Type `#` for tags and `/` for folders.
- The save sheet asks for your own fields first (like Big Idea), has a tag picker with your real tags, and a folder picker.
- If the video is already in your vault, saving asks: update it, make a new note, or pick another note. Updating only adds. It never changes what you wrote.
- Export your templates as a Web Clipper settings file. It imports back into the browser clipper.

### Updates
- Update center in Settings. Tap Install once. Android asks for approval first, the download runs in the app with a progress bar, and the install follows. Android 14 and up approve before the download ends. Older phones ask after it.
- Works with a private repo if you add an access token under Update sources.
- Self-healing playback: every day a workflow checks for a newer NewPipe Extractor. If there is one, it runs the tests and a full build. If they pass, it makes a patch release by itself. The app shows an "Update ready" pop-up when it finds the release. If a video fails to play, the app also checks for a fix right away. Piped and Invidious servers are read live, so they heal without an update.
- For a private repo the app needs an access token (Settings, Updates, Update sources) to see releases.

- Install with Android's installer, with Shizuku, or with Wireless ADB. Pair wireless debugging once in Settings, Updates. After that the app reconnects, can turn wireless debugging back on by itself, and installs updates with no prompt. If Shizuku or ADB fails, the normal Android installer takes over.

### Reading flow
- Follow-along: if you scroll away from the spoken word, a "Sync to where it is" pop-up appears. Tap it and the text glides back, with a small spin on the icon.
- Notes on highlights: right after you colour a highlight, a note bar opens. Type a note, change the colour, play from it, or remove it. Tap any highlighted word later to reopen the bar.

### Home
- Tabs (Add, Library, Vault). Choose which ones show in Settings, Look.
- Library layouts: list, grid, compact.
- Pull to refresh with a pixel character: ghost, heart, star or invader. It refreshes your vaults and checks for updates.

### Look
- Fourteen palettes and a palette maker (one colour plus a style, with a preview in every mode).
- Light, Dusk, Dark and OLED.
- Use your own font for the transcript, or for the whole app.

### Not tested yet
This build passes the unit tests and live stream tests on a PC. It has not been run on a phone by the author. Report anything odd.
