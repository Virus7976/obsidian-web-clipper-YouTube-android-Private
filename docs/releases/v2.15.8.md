# ObsiTube v2.15.8

## Templater templates import

- Pick a Templater template with **From my vault**. ObsiTube reads its script. It does not run it.
- Questions the video can answer fill themselves in. For example: Author, URL, Date published.
- `tp.date.now(...)` becomes the clip date, in the same format.
- `tp.file.rename(...)` sets the note name. `tp.file.move("Folder/" ...)` sets the folder.
- Questions the video cannot answer are asked when you save.
- Your template file is never rewritten where it has Templater tags.

## A cleaner top bar

- Listening mode is one bar: title and channel, the seek bar with times, then Video, back 10, play, forward 10 and speed.
- The ⋮ menu has quick tiles: Find, Follow, Text only, and Video or Listen.
- The **Video** button is always there. If the saved sound has no picture, ObsiTube looks for a source with one.
