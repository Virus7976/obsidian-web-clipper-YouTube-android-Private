# v2.8.0

**Highlights screen**
- New **Highlights** chip on the Home page, next to Library, Feed and Queue. It shows how many you have.
- Every highlight and timed note in one list. Each card shows the words, your note, the video and the time, with a colour edge.
- Search at the top finds a word in the highlight, in your note, or in the video name. The matched words are marked.
- **Newest** or **By video**. By video groups the cards under each video's title, in the order they happen in the video.
- Narrow it with **With notes** and the six colour dots. Tap a dot again to turn it off.
- Tap a card to open the video at that spot. Long ones show 6 lines with Show more.
- The three-dot menu on a card: Copy, Copy as a quote (with the video and time), Delete. A delete shows Undo.
- A new search or filter always starts at the top.
