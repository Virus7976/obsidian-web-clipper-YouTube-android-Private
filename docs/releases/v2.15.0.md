## ObsiTube 2.15.0

Marginalia is now ObsiTube, with a purple crystal icon.

**Templates from your vault**
- Settings > Obsidian > From my vault. Pick a note and it becomes a clip template.
- Each property finds its own data from its name and your vault's property types.
- Tap a property to set its type: Automatic, Checkbox, Date, Date & time, List, Number or Text.
- Choose what goes in it: Ask me, Video data, Always the same or Leave empty.
- The list of video data shows real examples from your last video.
- Pick the same note again to update the template. Your choices stay.

**Before it saves**
- Properties set to Ask me open a box after you tap save.
- Dates get a calendar, lists get chips and vault search, checkboxes get a switch.
- Use video data fills a box with one tap.

**Also new**
- Notes that use `url:` instead of `source:` are found again, so a clip does not make a copy.
- More video data: minutes, seconds, highlight count, chapters, tags and category.
- A purple accent colour.

Auto-save does not ask. It uses the answers from your last clip of that video.
