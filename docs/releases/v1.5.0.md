New
- Lock screen player, built for this app. When the screen turns off while audio plays, a player shows over the lock screen: big cover on a blurred background, title, the words being spoken, a seek bar, back 10, play or pause, forward 10, a Highlight button, clock and date. Swipe up to unlock.
- Make it yours in Settings > Lock screen: style (Cover, Minimal, Words), background (blurred cover, theme colour, black), clock, spoken words, Highlight button. "Preview now" shows it right away.
- Android needs "display over other apps" for the lock screen to appear by itself. Settings has a button for it and shows the status.
- Clipping a video again brings back what you filled in last time: template, name, folder, vault, Big Idea and other fields, and tags.

Fixed
- Notification: back 10 and forward 10 now sit in the back and forward slots.
- Full screen: a stray tap on a word no longer jumps the audio.

Not tested on a phone by the author. The lock screen is the riskiest part. Samsung and other makers handle lock screen apps in their own way.
