# v2.5.0

Touch lock, so a phone in your pocket cannot skip or pause by accident.

- New **Lock** button on the notification and lock screen card. One tap locks.
- New **Lock** button on the lock screen player. A dark cover blocks every touch. Hold the pill to unlock.
- While locked, the notification, the lock screen card and headset buttons cannot pause, skip, seek or highlight.
- The lock clears when you unlock the phone, or when playback stops.
- Unlock is not on the notification on purpose. A stray tap in a pocket would undo the lock.
