# ObsiTube v2.15.16

## Saving to Obsidian

- Saving now asks for every empty field you fill by hand, and for tags. A note saved without them is hard to find later.
- Auto-save asks too. When a video ends and its note is new, the save sheet opens so you can add your details. With the app in the background, a notification asks instead.
- Fixed: "Add to Obsidian" could open your vault and never make the note. ObsiTube now writes the note into your vault folder itself, then opens the vault.
- The first time, it asks you to pick your vault folder. You do this once.
- The message after saving shows where the note went.
