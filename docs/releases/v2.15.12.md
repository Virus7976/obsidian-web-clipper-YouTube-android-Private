# ObsiTube v2.15.12

## Smoother screen changes

- Screens slide like on an iPhone. A new screen slides in from the right, over the one before. Back slides it away to the right.
- The video picture now moves with its page. Before, it lagged and slid on its own.
- Changing tab fades the new page in, with a small slide from the side of the tab you left.

## Safer saving

- Your library, highlights and settings are now forced onto the disk before they replace the old file.
- The app keeps the save before it. If the phone dies mid-save and leaves an empty file, the app reads the earlier save instead of starting empty.
