## ObsiTube 2.15.6

- A tapped highlight now has pull handles: a knob above its start and a knob below its end.
- Drag a knob to move that end, one word at a time. The page scrolls when you reach the edge.
- The two ends can never pass each other, so a highlight always keeps at least one word.
- A tap or a slip on the text can no longer shrink or erase the highlight you are changing.
