A full check of the app. This version fixes bugs and removes clutter.

**Playback**
- Pause on mute works now. It did nothing before.
- A video you finished starts from the beginning.
- Your place is saved all the time, also when the reader is closed or the queue moves on.
- A failed video keeps its place when you tap Try again.
- A paused video stays paused when the app changes its source.
- Opening the app unlocks the controls, as the notification says.

**Reading**
- The transcript opens at your place, not at the first line.
- Playback pauses only while you type a note, not each time you make a highlight.
- Back closes the note bar and saves the note.
- Deleting a highlight now has Undo.

**Going back**
- Back keeps your place on the home screen: tab, playlist, search and scroll.
- Back from a video returns to the channel as you left it.
- Removing a video from the reader now asks first. It also stops the video and takes it out of playlists.

**Obsidian**
- Notes written after a clip now reach the existing note.
- Timed notes now reach the existing note.
- Auto-save no longer runs twice for one video.

**Look and feel**
- Lists no longer run into the clock when you scroll.
- Every sheet now scrolls without the stretch glitch.
- Library rows have more room for the title. Pin is in the three-dot menu.
- Colour dots are easier to tap and fit on small phones.
- Removed the swipe hint, the shapes on the empty library and See all on short rows.
- The app no longer redraws the home list and reader five times a second during playback.

youtu.be links can now open in the app.
