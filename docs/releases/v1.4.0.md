New
- Full screen reading. Tap the full-screen icon in the reader (or ⋮ > Full screen). The top row, player, bottom bar, status bar and navigation bar all go away. You can still select and highlight, tap a word to jump, and use the Sync pop-up. Leave with the Back button or the faint icon in the top corner. Swipe from the screen edge to peek at the system bars.

Fixed
- Tapping the now-playing bar no longer restarts the audio for a second. Re-opening a video that already plays keeps it playing, and the picture stays off unless you ask.
- The reader slides in smoothly. The long transcript now loads after the slide, with a short fade.
- Back 10 and forward 10 on the media notification instead of "back to start".

Polish
- The now-playing bar shows a thin progress line.
- Tabs show counts: Playlists · 2, Queue · 3.
