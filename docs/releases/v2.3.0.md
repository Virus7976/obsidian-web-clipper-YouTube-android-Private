# 2.3.0

**Watch later and your own playlists**
- New Watch later button in every video menu. It makes the Watch later playlist the first time you use it.
- Make your own playlists. Use New playlist in a video's menu, or on the Playlists page.
- Each playlist has its own switch: Remove videos as I watch them. When a video plays to the end, or almost to the end, it leaves that playlist. Watch later has it on. Your own playlists start with it off.
- The video menu always shows your playlists now, even when you have not imported any.

**Looking at a menu no longer adds the video**
- Tap and hold a video from the feed or a channel to see its menu. The video joins your library only when you pick something that needs it, or tap Add to library. The check mark now means it is really in your library.
- Obsidian and Go to channel moved to a row under the buttons.
