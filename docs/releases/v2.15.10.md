# ObsiTube v2.15.10

## Tabs at the bottom

- Library, Feed and Queue are now a floating bar at the bottom, with an icon and a name each.
- The top of the page shows the page's name, large, with Add, Search and Settings.
- The playing video rests just above the tabs.

## One listening bar

- Listening, there is one small bar with the thumbnail, the time and play.
- One tap on it opens the seek bar and skip buttons. Before, the first tap only swapped it for a second small bar.
