# v2.13.0

Every part of the app had to answer one question: why is this here, and why here?

Reading
- Highlights now sit tight around the words, with soft corners. The spoken word glides along the line.
- A highlight with a note gets a small tick in the margin, so you can see there is something to read.
- Text fades at the top and bottom of the page. No line is cut in half against a bar.
- A jump of more than 30 seconds shows "Back to 9:41" for a few seconds, so a stray tap never loses your place.
- The Clip button is loud only when you marked something that is not in Obsidian yet. It says "Clip 2 new" or "Clipped".
- "Remove highlight" only shows when there is a highlight to remove.

Watching
- While a video plays, only Back and More stay on the picture. The rest come back when you tap.
- Double tap the left or right of the picture to skip 10 seconds.
- New: watch full screen, sideways.

The menu
- The reader menu went from 10 items to 6. The rest are in the same sheet the library uses.
- "Full screen" for text is now "Only the text", so it is not mixed up with watching.

Home
- The tabs are the title, so the top is one row.
- One search, the icon at the top. A box only shows on a long list.
- The video you were watching comes first, with how much is left.
- The same video no longer shows twice. One video in a row now takes the whole line.
- The bar at the bottom matches the cards and shows the video as a wide picture.

Other
- One highlight icon everywhere. The old one looked like a torch.
- The Highlights page drops what says nothing when all your highlights are from one video.
- Save to Obsidian keeps its button on screen, however long the form is.
- "Open note" only shows when there is a note.
