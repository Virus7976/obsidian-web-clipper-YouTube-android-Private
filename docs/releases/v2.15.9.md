# ObsiTube v2.15.9

## A smaller listening bar

- Listening, the top is one small row: back, title, time, play and ⋮, with a thin progress line.
- Tap the title to open the seek bar, back 10, forward 10 and speed. They close by themselves after a few seconds.
- Switch to the video from the ⋮ menu.

## Cleaner Obsidian settings

- The Obsidian page is now short cards: Templates, Vaults, Saving a clip, Added to every clip, Note names.
- Templates come first. Add one from your vault or blank. Import, export and reset are in the ⋮ next to Templates.
- Each vault has its own ⋮ for Reindex, Choose its folder and Remove. Tap a vault to make it the default.

## A simpler template screen

- The name is the title. Tap it to rename.
- Three cards: Properties, Saving and Note text. Each row shows its value. Tap it to change it.
- "If the note is already there" uses plain words: Make a new note, Replace the note, Add to the end, Add to the top.
- The list of variables and filters shows only when you edit the note text.
