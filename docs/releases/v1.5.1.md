- Highlight syntax now matches your Obsidian plugin: `==` then a coloured circle then the text then `==`. For example a red one is `==` + red circle + `sdf` + `==`.
- Colours: Yellow, Red, Green, Blue, Purple and a new Orange. Highlights you already made keep their colour.
- The old `<mark style="background: ...">` form is gone.

Not tested on a phone by the author.
