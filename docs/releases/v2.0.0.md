# 2.0.0

**Channels and a feed**
- Follow a channel and its newest videos show in the new Feed tab. Pull down to check again.
- Tap a channel to see all its videos, and tap a video to read it.
- Find a channel by searching its name, or open a video's menu and choose Go to channel. The reader's menu has it too.

**One place to add**
- The Add page is gone. Tap Add at the top. A pop-up takes a video link, a playlist link, a channel link, or words to search for.
- A video link opens in the reader at once. There is no second button to reach under the keyboard.

**Playlists live in the library**
- The Playlists tab is gone. Your playlists sit at the top of the Library. Tap one to open it.

**Sharing from YouTube**
- A shared video now opens in the reader every time, even when the reader or another screen is already open.
- You can also share a playlist or a channel from YouTube.

Your tabs reset once to Library, Feed and Queue. You can change them in Settings > Look.
