Save sheet, video sheet and polish.

Save sheet
- When a video is already in your vault, the sheet now fills in what that note has: Big Idea, tags and the other fields. Add to it, then choose "Update it". If you change a box, the note changes too. Tags are only added, never removed.
- It is a setting: Settings > Obsidian > "Fill the sheet from my note". On by default.
- A video header at the top, calmer banner, rounder fields and sections.

Video sheet (tap and hold, or the three dots)
- New look: the video at the top, a row of big buttons (Play next, Queue, Save audio, Obsidian), then Category and Tags with suggestions you can scroll, then Playlists.
- Remove asks first.

Not tested on a phone by the author.
