Lock screen, notification and vault fixes. This also includes everything in 1.5.2.

Lock screen
- Pure black by default, so an OLED screen lights almost nothing. The close X is gone.
- Page layout: the transcript fills the screen like a page. Select words and highlight them right on the lock screen. It follows the audio, and a Sync button appears if you scroll away.
- Drag the bar above the controls down to hide them and read. Drag it up to bring them back.
- Minimal layout, like a music app's lock screen: a centred clock, the title with back 10 and forward 10 beside it, a seek bar, and the controls. The corner button flips between the words and minimal.
- Back 10, play or pause, forward 10, Highlight and Unlock are on the controls.
- Settings > Lock screen: layout (page, cover and page, minimal), background (black, blurred cover, theme colour), accent, text size, clock (small or large), title, seek bar, Highlight button, follow along, start with controls hidden, keep the screen on.

Notification
- Back 10 and forward 10 are now custom actions, the same kind as Highlight, which your phone already shows. Phone makers skip the standard seek buttons.

Vault (from 1.5.2)
- Clip uses the vault you chose in Settings. A vault name inside a template is only a fallback.
- A clip never adds a vault to your list. Vault names that older versions added by mistake are removed once.

Not tested on a phone by the author, except the first lock screen you tried.
