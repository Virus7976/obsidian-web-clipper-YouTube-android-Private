# v2.6.0

Search, review, and Obsidian saving that does itself.

**Search everything**
- New search button at the top of Home. One box finds videos, the words you highlighted, your timed notes, playlists and channels.
- Skipped letters are forgiven in names, like Obsidian: "mrcs stc" finds "Marcus Aurelius on Stoic Habits".
- Several words must all match. `#tag` looks only at tags and categories.
- A highlight result opens the reader at the place it came from.
- Empty box shows your recent searches and your newest highlights.
- Every list search forgives skipped letters in titles now. The highlights list in the reader got a search box too.

**Review mode**
- A highlight comes back a day after you make it, then after 3, 7, 16, 35, 90 and 180 days each time you keep it.
- Swipe right or press Keep. Swipe left or press Drop. A drop only stops it showing in Review. The highlight stays.
- A Review card shows on the Library page when some are ready.
- Settings > Review: a daily reminder with one old highlight, at a time you pick.

**Save to Obsidian by itself**
- Settings > Obsidian: "Save by itself when a video ends". Only for videos with highlights.
- Uses the template, folder and tags of your last clip. Adds to your note when there is one. Never opens Obsidian.

**Video menu**
- Opens as a half drawer with the most used things: the four tiles and your playlists. Swipe up for links, category, tags, pin and remove.
- Playlists moved to the top, where you reach for them.
