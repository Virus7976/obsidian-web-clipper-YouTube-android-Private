# v2.7.0

**Review**
- Undo: every swipe (keep, drop, skip) shows an Undo pill for 6 seconds. It puts the card back exactly as it was.
- Settings > Review > "Highlights in one round": 5, 10, 20 or no limit.
- A drop only stops a highlight coming back to Review. It stays in the video and in your Obsidian note.

**Notes**
- Settings > Playback > "Pause while I type a note": playback waits while you write a note on a highlight, then carries on. On by default.

**Smoother**
- The video menu no longer jumps when you swipe it up. The hint under the playlists now fades instead of vanishing.

**Search**
- A new search now starts at the top of the list, where the search box is. Before, the list kept its old scroll place and the box ended up out of sight. Same on channel pages.
