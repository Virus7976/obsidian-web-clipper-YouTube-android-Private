# 2.1.1

- The transcript no longer shows caption marks such as `>>` and `[music]`. They are hidden, not deleted, so your saved highlights stay in place. Highlighted text saved to your note leaves them out too.
- Scrolling away from the spoken word now pauses follow-along at once, and the Sync button shows at once. The text stays where you put it.
- While you highlight or write a note, the text no longer jumps back to the spoken word.
