A new reader layout. The text gets the screen.

- The player folds into one small row when you scroll to read.
- Tap that row, or scroll to the very top, to bring the player back.
- The player also folds while you type a note or search.
- The video keeps playing while it folds.
- Controls are now centred, with the times beside the seek bar.
- The chapter you are in now shows in the bottom bar. Tap it to see all chapters.
- Sync is now a button in the top row. It no longer covers the text.
- Text no longer runs behind the phone's buttons.
- Highlights are softer on a dark screen.
- Tapping the notification now opens the video that is playing.
